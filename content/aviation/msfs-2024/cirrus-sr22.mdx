The MSFS 2024 Cirrus SR22T: a turbocharged four-plus-one-seat piston single with Perspective+ glass and a whole-airplane
parachute. Numbers come from the FAA type certificate (TCDS A00009CH) and the SR22T POH (P/N 13772-005). The
procedures follow that POH and were checked against Cirrus's SR22 Perspective+ POH (P/N 13772-006) and abbreviated
checklist (P/N 13728-014). Speeds are in KIAS (knots indicated airspeed) unless marked, and the sim's own quirks are
listed separately. Editions, the EFB, SimBrief, the
checklist panel and key bindings are in [sim basics](/aviation/msfs-2024/sim-basics/). Radio calls are in
[ATC comms](/aviation/atc-comms/). For a simpler trainer first, see the [Cessna 172](/aviation/msfs-2024/cessna-172/).

<Note kind="warning">For the simulator only. The checklists here are condensed paraphrases, not the Cirrus POH. Never
use them to fly a real airplane.</Note>

## Profile

Which SR22 is it? The sim ships the **SR22T** (turbo, Continental TSIO-550-K), not the normally aspirated SR22
(IO-550-N, 310 hp, 2,700 RPM). Asobo and Working Title built it as a G6 with Perspective+ avionics for MSFS 2020 Sim
Update 14 (December 2023), and MSFS 2024 carries it over. The edition lists call it "Cirrus SR22"; the SU14 announcement and the
MSFS 2024 feedback thread call it the SR22T.

| Item | Value | Notes and source |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| role | high-performance single-engine piston, IFR tourer | fixed gear, constant-speed prop, side yokes |
| engine | Continental TSIO-550-K, turbocharged, 315 hp at 2,500 RPM | TCDS; POH §1 |
| propeller | Hartzell 3-blade composite, 78 in, constant speed | TCDS; POH §1 |
| seats | 5 (4+1: two front, 2+1 rear) | TCDS, S/N 0268 and on |
| max takeoff weight | 3,600 lb; max zero-fuel 3,400 lb | TCDS (S/N 0442 and on); POH §2 |
| usable fuel | 92.0 US gal of 94.5 total (about 550 lb of 100LL) | TCDS; POH §1 |
| typical burn | 18.3 / 16.4 / 14.6 / 12.7 gal/h at 85 / 75 / 65 / 55% power | POH §5 cruise table (best-economy mixture) |
| cruise | 213 KTAS at 25,000 ft, 85% power, ISA; 184 KTAS at 10,000 ft | POH §5; Cirrus spec page (213 KTAS) |
| ceiling | 25,000 ft MSL (maximum operating altitude) | TCDS; POH §2 |
| range | 812 NM at 25,000 ft, 85% power, 45-min IFR reserve[^1] | POH §5, full-power climb table |
| MSFS edition | Premium Deluxe | first-party aircraft list; Windows Central edition list |
| avionics | Cirrus Perspective+ by Garmin (G1000 NXi-based), GFC 700 autopilot | two large displays, FMS keypad |

**KTAS** is true airspeed: what the airplane actually does through the air, higher than KIAS at altitude. **Perspective+**
is Cirrus's name for its Garmin G1000 NXi integration.

## Speeds

Weight 3,600 lb unless noted. **V-speeds** are the standard reference speeds: Vr rotate, Vx best angle, Vy best rate,
Vfe max flap speed, Vno max structural cruise (yellow arc above), Vne never exceed, Vpd max demonstrated parachute
deployment.

| Speed | KIAS | Meaning and notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Vso | 64 | stall, flaps 100%, idle, most-forward CG (61 KCAS); bottom of the white arc |
| Vs (clean) | 74 | stall, flaps up, forward CG; bottom of the green arc |
| Vs (50%) | 70 | stall, flaps 50% |
| Vr | 77 | normal takeoff: rotate at 77–80, flaps 50% |
| obstacle speed | 85 | short-field climb speed at the 50 ft obstacle |
| flaps up | 90 | retract flaps 50% → UP on a normal takeoff |
| Vx | 88 | best angle, sea level and 10,000 ft |
| Vy | 103 | best rate at sea level; 102 at 10,000 ft |
| cruise climb | 120 | normal full-power, full-rich climb |
| best glide | 92 | all weights; about 8.6:1, 14.2 NM from 10,000 ft AGL[^2] |
| approach, flaps 100% | 80–85 | normal final; 85–90 with 50%, 90–95 flaps up |
| Vref (short field) | 79 | threshold speed, flaps 100% |
| go-around | 80–85 | full power, flaps 50% |
| Vfe | 150 / 110 | flaps 50% / 100% (older S/N below 0442: 119 / 104); no flaps above 17,500 ft |
| Vo | 140 | operating maneuvering speed at 3,600 lb; 133 at 3,400, 124 at 2,900, 112 at 2,400 |
| turbulent air | 140 / 123 | max recommended penetration speed at 3,600 / 2,900 lb |
| Vno | 176 | up to 17,500 ft, falling linearly to 150 at 25,000 ft |
| Vne | 205 | up to 17,500 ft, falling linearly to 175 at 25,000 ft |
| Vpd | 140 | CAPS: maximum demonstrated deployment speed |
| crosswind | 21 kt | maximum demonstrated, not a limit |

Load limits: +3.8 g / −1.9 g flaps up, +1.9 g / 0 g with flaps. Spins are not approved: a spin means CAPS.

Sim vs real: Working Title describes the SU14 rebuild as having "book-accurate" performance, so the POH numbers above
are the reference. Nobody has published a speed-by-speed comparison; treat any gap you find as a sim difference.

Perspective+ features the sim models (Working Title, SU14): stabilized-approach alerts, a full-screen engine page with
fuel-flow targets, a weight-and-balance page with a CG plot, trip planning (endurance, range, ETE, ETA), interactive
checklists, and TKS anti-ice fluid flow (TKS: fluid "weeping wing" ice protection).

| Key or page | Use |
| :--- | :--- |
| FPL (keypad) | flight plan page on the MFD; build or check the route |
| PROC (keypad) | load departure, arrival or approach; activate the approach |
| Direct-to (keypad) | direct to a waypoint |
| MFD engine page | fuel used and remaining, cyan target fuel flow, TIT, CHT |

**TIT** is turbine inlet temperature; **CHT** is cylinder head temperature; **MAP** is manifold pressure in inHg.

Where the non-turbo Perspective+ book differs from the SR22T the sim models:

| Item | SR22T (13772-005) | Non-turbo SR22 (13772-006) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| engine | TSIO-550-K, 315 hp at 2,500 RPM, 36.0 inHg for takeoff | IO-550-N, 310 hp at 2,700 RPM (§1) |
| fuel pump switch | BOOST and HIGH BOOST/PRIME | BOOST and PRIME (§7) |

| cruise leaning | to the cyan target at 30.5 inHg or less, or 50–75 °F lean of peak TIT | by EGT: 75 °F rich of peak (best power) or 50 °F lean of peak (best economy) |

| cabin power | AVIONICS OFF; BAT 2 ON: PFD comes on, essential bus 23–25 V, flap light out; BAT 1 ON, avionics fan audible |

### Engine start

![SR22 center console, non-turbo drawing: power lever (8), mixture (10) with its CUTOFF stop, friction knob (9), fuel pump switch and the fuel selector below; set mixture FULL RICH and power FULL FORWARD, then open the power lever about ¼ inch to start](/images/aviation/cirrus-sr22/engine-controls.webp)
*Image: Cirrus Aircraft, SR22 Perspective+ POH/AFM (P/N 13772-006) fig. 7-7, © Cirrus Aircraft, all rights reserved, reproduced for reference.*

| Item | Action / value |
| :--- | :--- |
| mixture | FULL RICH |
| power lever | FULL FORWARD |
| fuel pump | BOOST; first start of the day or cold: HIGH BOOST/PRIME for about 2 s first; warm engine: no prime |
| prop area | clear |
| power lever | open about ¼ inch |
| ignition key | START; release when it runs; crank ≤ 10 s, then 20 s rest |
| mixture | lean until RPM peaks; leave it there for taxi and run-up |
| power | back to about 1,000 RPM |
| oil pressure | rising within 30 s (60 s when very cold), else shut down |
| ALT 1, ALT 2, AVIONICS | ON |
| engine, amps | monitor the engine page; check the ammeter |

Flooded (weak firing, black smoke): pump OFF, mixture full lean, power full open, crank; when it fires, release the key,
pull the power back and slowly enrich.

### Before takeoff

![Flap control at the bottom of the center stack: the airfoil-shaped lever at the right of the panel moves between UP, 50% and 100%; set 50% for takeoff and check the matching position light](/images/aviation/cirrus-sr22/flap-switch.webp)

| Item | Action / value |
| :--- | :--- |
| doors, CAPS, belts | latched; CAPS pin removed; belts and harnesses secure |
| air conditioner | as desired, never RECIRC in flight |
| fuel | confirm quantity; fullest tank; pump BOOST; mixture FULL RICH (the turbo holds 36 inHg at any field elevation) |
| flaps | 50%; check the light |
| transponder, autopilot, nav | set; autopilot checked; departure loaded |
| run-up | brakes held, 1,700 RPM |
| alternators | pitot heat, nav lights and landing light ON as a load; both ALT cautions out, both show positive amps; voltage good |
| pitot heat | ON in visible moisture or at 5 °C (41 °F) or colder |
| magnetos | R then L, back to BOTH each time: drop ≤ 150 RPM each, difference ≤ 75 RPM |
| back to 1,000 RPM | engine parameters good; instruments, HSI and altimeter set; controls free and correct; trim at takeoff |

### Takeoff

![SR22 MFD engine page, non-turbo drawing: percent power (1), RPM (3), manifold pressure (5) and oil (6) gauges across the top, CHT (2) and EGT (4) bars below; at full power the SR22T shows about 36 inHg and 2,500 RPM](/images/aviation/cirrus-sr22/engine-instruments.webp)
*Image: Cirrus Aircraft, SR22 Perspective+ POH/AFM (P/N 13772-006) fig. 7-7, © Cirrus Aircraft, all rights reserved, reproduced for reference.*

| Item | Action / value |
| :--- | :--- |
| brakes | release; steer with rudder (the real nosewheel castors; brakes steer at low speed) |
| power | full forward: about 36.0 inHg and 2,500 RPM, fuel flow in the green arc |
| MAP check | up to 37.0 inHg is fine with cold oil; above 37.0, pull back |
| rotate | 77–80 KIAS |
| flaps | UP at 90 KIAS |
| short field | flaps 50%, hold brakes, full power, check the engine, release, rotate 77, climb at 85 over the obstacle |

### Climb

| Item | Action / value |
| :--- | :--- |
| oxygen | as required |
| full-power climb | 120 KIAS, full power, flaps up, mixture keeping fuel flow in the green arc, pump BOOST |
| max climb | Vy 103 (Vx 88 for obstacles), briefly: slow climbs heat the cylinders |
| CHT | keep the hottest ≤ 420 °F by lowering the nose; 460 °F is the red line |
| cruise climb (lean of peak) | 30.5 inHg, lean to the cyan target, ≥ 120 KIAS, pump BOOST (130–140 KIAS gives 600–700 fpm) |
| above 18,000 ft | hot fuel may vapor-lock: HIGH BOOST/PRIME if fuel flow wobbles |

### Cruise

![MFD fuel-flow gauge (1) with the fuel quantity and fuel calculation boxes below: in cruise, lean until the needle sits on the cyan target cue, then watch fuel remaining, range and the tank balance](/images/aviation/cirrus-sr22/fuel-flow.webp)
*Image: Cirrus Aircraft, SR22 Perspective+ POH/AFM (P/N 13772-006) fig. 7-9, © Cirrus Aircraft, all rights reserved, reproduced for reference.*

| Item | Action / value |
| :--- | :--- |
| oxygen, altitude | as required; cruise altitude established |
| power | 30.5 inHg or less (leaning is approved only at or below 30.5) |
| fuel pump | BOOST for 30 min after level-off, then OFF or as needed; BOOST for maneuvers and every tank switch |
| mixture | lean to the cyan target fuel flow (lean of peak), or 50–75 °F lean of peak TIT |
| CHT | ≤ 420 °F; 0.5 gal/h leaner cuts CHT about 15 °F |
| fuel balance | swap tanks regularly: FUEL IMBALANCE advisory over 8 gal, caution over 10, warning over 12 |
| high altitude | MAP never below 15 inHg above 18,000 ft |

**Lean of peak (LOP)**: leaner than the mixture giving peak exhaust temperature; cooler and more economical, slightly
slower.

## Flying it in the sim

![Perspective+ FMS keyboard on the center stack: FPL (23) for the flight plan, PROC (26) for procedures, Direct-to (24), the FMS knob (29), COM and NAV tuning keys and XPDR (30) for the transponder](/images/aviation/cirrus-sr22/fms-keyboard.webp)
*Image: Cirrus Aircraft, SR22 Perspective+ POH/AFM (P/N 13772-006) fig. 7-18, © Cirrus Aircraft, all rights reserved, reproduced for reference.*

A typical IFR trip in the SR22T. Plan and send the route from the EFB or SimBrief as in
[sim basics](/aviation/msfs-2024/sim-basics/#flight-planning-efb-and-simbrief); the clearance and radio calls are in
[IFR departure](/aviation/atc-comms/ifr-departure/).

| Step | What to do |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1. load | send the EFB plan to the avionics; check it on the MFD FPL page |
| 2. procedures | PROC: departure, then arrival and approach once you know the runway |
| 3. altitudes | set the altitude preselect to the first cleared altitude; add VNAV constraints on the FPL page |
| 4. takeoff | hand-fly, then AP on after climb-out |
| 5. climb | FLC 120 KIAS full rich, or 130–140 lean of peak |
| 6. level | ALT captures at the preselect; set cruise power and lean |
| 7. descent | set the lower altitude first, then VNV follows the computed path (VNAV: vertical navigation) |
| 8. approach | activate the approach, APR, slow to 90–95, then configure |

Autopilot (GFC 700) modes on the GMC 707 mode controller:

| Mode | Does |
| :--- | :--- |
| AP | engages the autopilot in the modes shown at the top of the PFD |
| FD | flight director cues only |
| HDG | follows the heading bug |
| NAV | follows the active GPS or VOR/LOC course |
| APR | arms the approach: LOC and glideslope, or GPS lateral and vertical guidance (LPV, LNAV/VNAV) |
| ALT | holds the current altitude |
| VS | holds a vertical speed |
| FLC | flight level change: pitches to hold an airspeed while power sets climb or descent rate |
| VNV | follows the FMS vertical path and altitude constraints |
| LVL | blue button: wings level and zero vertical speed; cancels other modes; your "get me out of here" button |

Tips:

- There is no autothrottle: FLC climbs need full power set by hand, and descents need power reduced by hand.
- The 3-to-1 rule checks the VNAV top of descent (TOD): 3 NM per 1,000 ft to lose, so 9,000 ft needs about 27 NM.
- ILS or LPV: approach active, NAV source correct, APR armed before the final approach fix.

## Emergencies

Paraphrased from the SR22T POH (13772-005) section 3 and checked against the Perspective+ POH (13772-006) section 3
(pp. 3-11 to 3-43), section 3A for cautions (pp. 3A-9 to 3A-14) and section 10 for CAPS (pp. 10-5 to 10-8). Rows
marked "turbo only" exist only in the SR22T book. Those POH sections are the full reference.

| Emergency | Memory items and notes |
| :--- | :--- |
| engine failure on takeoff | best glide or landing speed; mixture CUTOFF, fuel selector OFF, ignition OFF; flaps as needed; if time allows: power IDLE, pump OFF, belts, BAT and ALT switches OFF last (the PFD goes dark) |
| engine failure below 500 ft AGL | land straight ahead, turning only to miss obstacles |
| engine failure 500–2,000 ft AGL | CAPS is most likely the safest option |
| engine failure in flight | 92 KIAS; switch tanks; ignition BOTH; pump BOOST; power ½; mixture from cutoff, slowly forward; no restart → CAPS or forced landing |
| engine airstart | BAT switches ON, power ½ inch, mixture rich as needed, switch tanks, ignition BOTH, pump BOOST, ALT switches OFF, starter only if the prop has stopped, power up slowly, ALT switches ON |
| forced landing without power | 92 KIAS; Mayday on 121.5, squawk 7700; power IDLE, mixture CUTOFF, fuel OFF, ignition OFF, pump OFF; flaps 100% once the field is made; belts; BAT and ALT OFF |
| partial power loss | pump BOOST, switch tanks, check the mixture, sweep the power lever, alternate air ON, ignition BOTH then L then R; land as soon as practicable |
| sudden loss of MAP (turbo only) | power to the minimum for level flight, mixture for 1,300–1,400 °F EGT, descend to a safe altitude, divert, land as soon as possible; watch oil pressure |
| MAN PRESSURE warning (turbo only) | power below 36.5 inHg; surging → 30.5 inHg or less, fuel flow at the top of the green arc |
| TIT warning (turbo only) | mixture to the top of the green arc, ignition on BOTH; still high → less power, land as soon as practicable |
| OIL PRESS warning | check the gauge; low or high → minimum power for level flight, land as soon as possible, expect the engine to quit |
| engine fire in flight | mixture CUTOFF, pump OFF, fuel OFF, cabin airflow OFF, power IDLE, ignition OFF; land as soon as possible; no restart |
| engine fire during start | mixture CUTOFF, pump OFF, fuel OFF, power forward, keep cranking to pull the fire into the engine |
| cabin fire | BAT 1, ALT 1, ALT 2 OFF as needed (BAT 2 keeps the PFD about 30 min), fire extinguisher, AVIONICS and other switches OFF; land as soon as possible |
| emergency descent | power IDLE, mixture as needed, up to 205 KIAS (176 in turbulence) |
| spin, or an unusual attitude after a stall | CAPS at once; no other spin recovery is approved |
| alternator failure (ALT 1 or 2 caution) | check and set its breaker, cycle the switch once; no reset → that ALT OFF; ALT 1 lost → shed non-essential load; avoid IMC and night |
| high bus voltage (M BUS warning, over 32 V) | cycle that ALT switch; still over 32 V → that ALT OFF, and don't reset it |
| ESS BUS warning, under 24.5 V | both alternators failed: run both ALT procedures, shed load (landing light, pitot heat, lights, COM 2), land as soon as practicable on battery |
| low fuel | 9 gal or less in total (FUEL QTY in the SR22T book, FUEL LOW TOTAL in the Perspective+ book): land as soon as practicable |
| FUEL LOW LEFT or RIGHT (Perspective+ book) | under 1 gal in that tank: pump BOOST, select the other tank |
| fuel imbalance | warning over 12 gal: pump BOOST, select the fuller tank |

**CAPS** (Cirrus Airframe Parachute System) lowers the whole airplane under a parachute.

| Item | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| handle | remove the cover, pull the T-handle straight down with both hands, steady like a chin-up (a jerk makes it heavier) |
| max demonstrated speed | 140 KIAS (Vpd); slow down first if you can, but pull regardless if ground impact is imminent |
| altitude | no minimum is set; altitude loss from level flight has been under 400 ft; aim for ≥ 2,000 ft AGL |
| attitude | wings level and upright if at all possible |
| mandatory | any spin, at any altitude |
| other cases | midair collision, structural failure, loss of control, no landing surface, pilot incapacitation |
| after the pull | mixture CUTOFF, fuel OFF, pump OFF, masters OFF after radio calls, ignition OFF, ELT ON, belts tight, loose items secured, brace; get out and move upwind |
| descent | under 1,700 fpm, drifting with the surface wind (POH 13772-006 p. 10-8) |
| touchdown | about like a drop from 13 ft; the airframe is likely written off |

### Safe Return and autoland

The sim's SR22T is a G6: it has **no** Safe Return. Real Cirrus added Garmin's Safe Return emergency autoland to the SR
series with the G7+ (2025). For an automatic emergency landing in MSFS, use the
[Vision Jet](/aviation/msfs-2024/vision-jet/).

For radio calls in an emergency (Mayday, 7700), see [emergencies and lost comms](/aviation/atc-comms/emergencies-lost-comms/).

## Recipes

### Cold and dark to takeoff

Start from a parking spot with everything off.

```text
BAT 1, BAT 2 ON ........... check volts; strobes ON
Mixture FULL RICH, power FULL FWD
Pump HIGH BOOST/PRIME 2 s (cold) -> BOOST
Power 1/4 in open, key START
Lean for peak RPM, 1,000 RPM, oil pressure up
ALT 1, ALT 2, AVIONICS ON
EFB plan -> avionics, check FPL, altimeter, transponder
Flaps UP, taxi <= 1,000 RPM
Run-up 1,700 RPM: alternators, mags (<= 150 drop, <= 75 diff)
Fullest tank, pump BOOST, mixture FULL RICH, flaps 50%
Lineup: full power ~36 inHg, rotate 77-80, flaps UP at 90
```

### Quick IFR setup

For a flight you've already planned in the EFB.

```text
EFB -> send to avionics; FPL page: check every waypoint
PROC: departure; altitude preselect = first cleared alt
Takeoff by hand; AP on; NAV + FLC 120 KIAS
Level: 30.5 inHg or less, lean to the cyan target
Arrival: PROC -> arrival + approach; VNAV constraints
Descent: preselect lower altitude, VNV, power back
Approach: activate, APR, 90-95 KIAS, flaps 50% then 100%
```

### Lean-of-peak cruise

After leveling off.

```text
Power 30.5 inHg or less; RPM 2,500
Wait for the cyan target cue on the fuel-flow gauge
Pull mixture slowly to the cue (or 50-75 F lean of peak TIT)
Rough running -> a touch richer
Any CHT above 420 F -> 0.5 gal/h leaner (about -15 F)
```

### Engine-out glide practice

Set a failure in the sim's [failures menu](/aviation/msfs-2024/sim-basics/#failures) at 5,000 ft AGL or higher.

```text
Pitch for 92 KIAS immediately; trim
Nearest airport on the MFD; turn toward it
Fuel selector other tank, ignition BOTH, pump BOOST
Power 1/2, mixture from cutoff slowly forward
No restart: glide ~1.4 NM per 1,000 ft AGL
Decide by 2,000 ft AGL: field made, or CAPS
```

### CAPS practice (sim only)

To see the sim's parachute, not a technique to copy.

```text
Level at 5,000 ft AGL or higher, slow to about 100 KIAS
Ceiling: remove cover, pull the red T-handle
Mixture CUTOFF, fuel OFF, pump OFF, masters OFF
Watch the descent rate (compare with Sim quirks)
```
