Formulas are written in LaTeX between dollar signs. Inline: the roots of $ax^2 + bx + c = 0$ are
$x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}$. Display maths goes between double dollars:

$$
e^{i\pi} + 1 = 0
$$

## Calculus

$$
\frac{d}{dx} x^n = n x^{n-1}
\qquad
\int_a^b f(x)\,dx = F(b) - F(a)
$$

## Linear algebra

$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{pmatrix},
\qquad
\det A = ad - bc
$$

## Probability

$$
P(A \mid B) = \frac{P(B \mid A)\,P(A)}{P(B)}
$$
