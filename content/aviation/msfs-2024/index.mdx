Aircraft profiles, checklists and autopilot workflows for five Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024 aircraft, plus
[sim basics](/aviation/msfs-2024/sim-basics/): editions, the EFB, keys, assists and avionics manuals. What to say
on the radio lives in [ATC comms](/aviation/atc-comms/fundamentals/). Numbers come from the manufacturer's POH and
the FAA type certificate. Where the sim differs from the real aircraft, the sheet says so.

<Note kind="warning">For flight simulation only. Real aircraft: use the manufacturer's POH/AFM and approved checklists.</Note>

| Aircraft | Edition | Avionics in the sim | Role | Difficulty |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| [Cessna 172 Skyhawk (G1000)](/aviation/msfs-2024/cessna-172/) | Standard | Garmin G1000 NXi with GFC 700 autopilot | 4-seat trainer, 180 hp | beginner |
| [Cessna 172 Classic](/aviation/msfs-2024/cessna-172-classic/) | Deluxe | six-pack gauges, GNS 530/430 or Avidyne IFD550/540, KAP 140 autopilot | the same 172S with round gauges | beginner; harder instrument scan |
| [Cirrus SR22T](/aviation/msfs-2024/cirrus-sr22/) | Premium Deluxe | Cirrus Perspective+ (G1000 NXi based) | fast turbocharged 4-seat piston with a parachute | intermediate |
| [Cirrus Vision Jet SF50](/aviation/msfs-2024/vision-jet/) | Standard | Perspective Touch+ (G3000 based) | single-engine personal jet | intermediate |
| [Cessna Citation Longitude](/aviation/msfs-2024/longitude/) | Premium Deluxe | Garmin G5000 with autothrottle | super-midsize business jet | advanced |

Each edition includes everything in the ones below it: Standard (70 aircraft), Deluxe (80), Premium Deluxe (95) and
Aviator (125). Start with the G1000 172, move to the Classic to learn the six-pack, then work up to the jets.
