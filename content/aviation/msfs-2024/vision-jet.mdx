The MSFS 2024 Cirrus Vision Jet: a single-engine, single-pilot personal jet with autothrottle, a whole-airplane
parachute and Safe Return emergency autoland. It is free in every edition. Limits come from the SF50 type certificate
(FAA A00018CH, mirrored by EASA.IM.A.615); other numbers come from Cirrus and published pilot reports, because the
SF50 flight manual isn't public. Speeds are KIAS unless marked. Editions, the EFB, SimBrief, the checklist panel and
key bindings are in [sim basics](/aviation/msfs-2024/sim-basics/); radio calls are in [ATC comms](/aviation/atc-comms/);
the piston sibling is the [SR22T](/aviation/msfs-2024/cirrus-sr22/).

<Note kind="warning">For the simulator only. These checklists are condensed paraphrases built from limits and pilot
reports, not the Cirrus AFM. Never use them to fly a real airplane.</Note>

## Profile

The sim's jet is FlightFX's "Vision Jet G2", shipped free in MSFS 2024 and maintained by Working Title in sim updates.
Cirrus has since moved on to the G2+ (2021) and G3 (2026), so current Cirrus spec pages quote slightly higher numbers.

| Item | Value | Notes and source |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| role | single-engine very light jet, pressurized, retractable gear | certified for one pilot |
| engine | Williams FJ33-5A turbofan, 1,846 lb takeoff thrust | TCDS; takeoff thrust limited to 5 min |
| seats | up to 7, minimum crew 1 | TCDS |
| max takeoff weight | 6,000 lb; ramp 6,040, landing 5,550, zero fuel 4,900 | TCDS |
| usable fuel | 2,000 lb (296 US gal of Jet A) | Cirrus spec page; Wikipedia (G2) |
| typical burn | about 60–64 gal/h (400–430 lb/h) at FL310 | Aviation Week and AOPA pilot reports |
| cruise | about 305 KTAS at FL310; about 311–313 KTAS max cruise lower down[^1] | AOPA (G2); FlightFX manual |
| ceiling | FL310 (31,000 ft); FL280 on early serials | TCDS: S/N 0008, 0089, 0094 and on |
| range | about 1,100–1,275 NM, depending on load and speed[^2] | AOPA (G2); Cirrus |
| pressurization | 7.1 psi differential: 8,000 ft cabin at FL310 | AOPA (G2 change from 6.4 psi) |
| MSFS edition | Standard (so in every edition) | first-party aircraft list; Fly Away Simulation; MSFS Addons |
| avionics | Cirrus Perspective Touch+ (Garmin G3000-based), autothrottle, ESP, Safe Return | two touch controllers |

**KTAS** is true airspeed. **FL310** is flight level 310: 31,000 ft on the standard altimeter setting (29.92 inHg),
used from 18,000 ft up. **ESP** (electronic stability and protection) nudges the controls back when you exceed bank,
pitch or speed limits.

## Speeds

Limits are from the TCDS. Takeoff and landing speeds depend on weight and are not published outside the AFM. Take them
from the EFB takeoff and landing performance (added in Sim Update 2); the table gives representative values from pilot
reports, clearly labeled.

| Speed | KIAS | Meaning and notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Vmo | 250 | maximum operating speed (red and white "barber pole" on the tape) |
| Mmo | 0.53 Mach | maximum operating Mach number; limits you before 250 KIAS up high |
| Vo | 150 | operating maneuvering speed: full control inputs only below this |
| Vfe 50% | 190 | max speed with flaps 50% |
| Vfe 100% | 150 | max speed with flaps 100% |
| Vlo extend / Vle | 210 | max speed to lower the gear, and with the gear down |
| Vlo retract | 150 | max speed to raise the gear |
| Vr (representative) | 85–90 | rotate; 85 at 5,958 lb (Aviation Week), 90 quoted by AOPA[^3] |
| flaps up (representative) | 115 | flaps 50% → 0 after takeoff (Aviation Week; other reports agree) |
| stall, full flaps | 67 | Wikipedia; FlightFX manual |
| approach | 140 by the initial fix | Cirrus technique: 140 KIAS and flaps 50%, then gear down and flaps 100% by the final fix |
| Vref (representative) | 75–85 | threshold speed; fly Vref + 10 on final[^3] |
| CAPS | 135 | max parachute deployment speed; the autopilot slows the jet to it if you pull faster |

**Vref** is the reference landing speed at the threshold, usually 1.3 × the stall speed in landing configuration.

## Cockpit & avionics

| Where | What's there |
| :--- | :--- |
| two 14-inch displays, landscape | PFD (flight instruments) on the left, MFD (map, engine and pressurization gauges) on the right |
| center, below the displays | two GTC touch controllers (Garmin Touch Controller): radios, flight plan, PROC, PERF, systems |
| autopilot mode controller | the usual Garmin modes: AP, FD, HDG, NAV, APR, ALT, VS, FLC, VNV |
| single throttle | TO at the top, a snap-to MCT (maximum continuous thrust) position, IDLE (FlightFX manual) |
| A/T and TOGA | an autothrottle arm control and a TOGA button (used in the reported flows below) |
| engine controls | ENGINE knob OFF → RUN, then a START button (FlightFX manual) |
| ceiling, between the front seats | CAPS T-handle between the crew oxygen masks (FLYER); the red Safe Return button is also in the ceiling[^4] |

Autothrottle (A/T) modes on the real G2 and in the sim:

| Mode | Does |
| :--- | :--- |
| MAN | holds the speed you set with the speed control (thumbwheel or bug) |
| FMS | follows the flight plan's speed schedule for climb, cruise, descent and approach |
| MCT | throttle "snaps" to maximum continuous thrust so you don't over-temp the engine (FlightFX manual) |
| protection | A/T prevents over- and under-speeds together with ESP |

There are **no speedbrakes**: the gear (below 210 KIAS) and flaps are the only drag. Plan descents early.

## Normal procedures

Condensed from the TCDS limits, Cirrus technique in pilot reports and forum-shared flows for the sim; forum items are
marked "(reported)". There is no official manual for the 2024 version: FlightFX points 2024 users to the Microsoft
forums.

### Preflight

| Item | Action / value |
| :--- | :--- |
| weight and balance | EFB: takeoff ≤ 6,000 lb, zero fuel ≤ 4,900 lb, landing ≤ 5,550 lb, baggage ≤ 300 lb combined |
| fuel | up to 2,000 lb; plan about 400–430 lb/h up high plus reserve |
| flight plan | destination loaded in the FMS: the pressurization schedule reads the destination elevation from it |
| EFB performance | takeoff and landing data for the runway; note Vr and Vref |

### Before start

| Item | Action / value |
| :--- | :--- |
| battery, avionics | ON; displays up |
| parking brake | set |
| door, belts | closed, fastened |
| CAS | review the crew alerting messages (CAS: the white, amber and red text list) |

### Engine start

| Item | Action / value |
| :--- | :--- |
| throttle | IDLE |
| ENGINE knob | RUN |
| START button | press; the FADEC (full-authority digital engine control) runs the start |
| engine | watch N1, N2 and ITT (turbine temperature) rise and settle; CAS clear |
| generator, lights | on as needed |

### Before taxi / taxi

| Item | Action / value |
| :--- | :--- |
| altitude preselect | first cleared altitude (reported flow: set it before takeoff) |
| heading | sync the bug to the runway heading |
| flaps | 50% |
| trim | in the takeoff band (about +4° reported) |
| taxi | parking brake off, brakes checked, idle thrust is plenty |

### Before takeoff

| Item | Action / value |
| :--- | :--- |
| EFB speeds | Vr and flaps 50% confirmed |
| autothrottle | armed |
| TOGA | pressed on the runway (takeoff/go-around: flight director shows the takeoff pitch) |
| transponder, lights | ALT; landing, strobe, nav ON |

### Takeoff

| Item | Action / value |
| :--- | :--- |
| throttle | full forward to TO |
| rotate | at Vr (85–90), to about 5° first, then follow the flight director |
| gear | UP with positive rate, below 150 KIAS |
| flaps | UP at 115 KIAS |
| thrust | back to MCT when safe; takeoff thrust is limited to 5 min |
| autopilot | ON after about 400–500 ft AGL (reported) |

### Climb

| Item | Action / value |
| :--- | :--- |
| mode | FLC or VNV, with the A/T in FMS |
| heavy climb | about 28 min to FL310 at 5,958 lb, ISA+15 (Aviation Week) |
| cabin | pressurization is automatic; watch the cabin altitude on the MFD |
| altimeter | set standard (29.92) passing 18,000 ft |

### Cruise

| Item | Action / value |
| :--- | :--- |
| level | FL280–FL310 for best range |
| speed | about 305 KTAS at FL310 on 60–64 gal/h; Mmo 0.53 is the limit up high |
| cabin | about 8,000 ft at FL310 |
| fuel | compare FMS fuel at destination with your reserve |

### Descent

| Item | Action / value |
| :--- | :--- |
| plan | start early: no speedbrakes; the FMS top of descent plus the 3-to-1 rule |
| mode | VNV with the A/T in FMS, or FLC with power back |
| speed | at or under 250 KIAS; 250 or less below 10,000 ft (ATC speed rule) |
| extra drag | gear down below 210 KIAS if you are high |
| altimeter | local setting at 18,000 ft |

### Approach

| Item | Action / value |
| :--- | :--- |
| by the initial fix | 140 KIAS, flaps 50% |
| approach | loaded and activated; APR armed; minimums set |
| by the final fix | gear down, flaps 100% (≤ 150 KIAS), Vref + 10 |
| pattern | Cirrus recommends 1,500 ft AGL for the traffic pattern |

### Landing

| Item | Action / value |
| :--- | :--- |
| autopilot | expect it to disconnect by 400 ft AGL; hand-fly the last part (reported) |
| over the threshold | Vref, throttle to IDLE |
| touchdown | mains first, then brakes |
| go-around | generic: TOGA, full thrust, pitch to the flight director, flaps 50%, gear up with positive rate |

### After landing

| Item | Action / value |
| :--- | :--- |
| flaps | UP |
| lights, transponder | taxi light; transponder STBY |
| A/T | disarmed |

### Shutdown

| Item | Action / value |
| :--- | :--- |
| parking brake | set |
| throttle | IDLE |
| ENGINE knob | OFF |
| avionics, battery | OFF |

## Flying it in the sim

A typical IFR flight. Load the plan as in [sim basics](/aviation/msfs-2024/sim-basics/#flight-planning-efb-and-simbrief); calls are in
[IFR departure](/aviation/atc-comms/ifr-departure/) and [IFR en route and arrival](/aviation/atc-comms/ifr-enroute-arrival/).

| Step | What to do |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1. plan | EFB or SimBrief plan sent to the avionics; check it on the GTC Flight Plan page |
| 2. performance | EFB takeoff data for Vr; GTC PERF for weights if shown |
| 3. departure | GTC PROC: departure; altitude preselect set; heading synced |
| 4. takeoff | flaps 50%, A/T armed, TOGA, full thrust, rotate, gear up, flaps up at 115 |
| 5. climb | AP on; FLC or VNV; A/T FMS; MCT once clean |
| 6. cruise | FL280–FL310; A/T holds the FMS or manual speed |
| 7. descent | arrival and approach loaded early; preselect the lower altitude; VNV |
| 8. approach | 140 KIAS and flaps 50% by the initial fix; APR; gear and flaps 100% by the final fix |
| 9. landing | hand-fly from 400 ft; Vref + 10 on final, Vref at the threshold |

Autopilot modes:

| Mode | Does |
| :--- | :--- |
| HDG | follows the heading bug |
| NAV | follows the FMS or VOR/LOC course |
| APR | arms the approach: ILS, or GPS lateral and vertical guidance |
| ALT | holds altitude |
| VS | holds a vertical speed |
| FLC | flight level change: holds an airspeed by pitch while the A/T sets power |
| VNV | follows the FMS vertical path and altitude constraints (VNAV) |

Flight-level change: set the new level, press FLC, and the A/T moves the throttle to climb or descent power. For a
descent, the rule of thumb is 3 NM per 1,000 ft: FL310 to 3,000 ft needs about 84 NM.

## Emergencies

The SF50's emergency checklists are in the AFM, which isn't public. Items marked "generic" are standard jet technique,
not Cirrus text.

| Emergency | What to do |
| :--- | :--- |
| engine failure below 600 ft AGL | do **not** pull CAPS; land ahead with little maneuvering; no turn back (Cirrus strategy per the FAA FSB report) |
| engine failure 600–2,000 ft AGL | pull CAPS immediately |
| engine failure above 2,000 ft AGL | you have time: glide toward an airport and try a restart, or pull CAPS |
| engine fire | generic: throttle IDLE, ENGINE knob OFF, then land at once or CAPS; follow the CAS checklist |
| depressurization (CABIN ALTITUDE alert) | generic: oxygen mask, emergency descent to 10,000 ft or the minimum safe altitude, squawk 7700 |
| emergency descent | throttle IDLE, gear down below 210 KIAS for drag, up to 250 KIAS / Mach 0.53 |
| electrical | generic: follow the CAS message's checklist; shed load; land as soon as practical |

For the radio side (Mayday, 7700, lost comms), see [emergencies and lost comms](/aviation/atc-comms/emergencies-lost-comms/).

### CAPS

| Item | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| what | Cirrus Airframe Parachute System: a rocket pulls a parachute out of the nose and the jet descends under it |
| handle | the CAPS T-handle in the ceiling |
| max speed | 135 KIAS; pulled faster, the autopilot idles the engine and pitches up (up to 60°, usually about 30°) to slow down |
| timing | canopy out and stable within about 15–30 s of the pull |
| descent | about 1,560 fpm under the canopy (Twin & Turbine) |
| altitude rule | never below 600 ft AGL; 600–2,000 ft after an engine failure; above 2,000 ft by choice |
| in the sim | the handle works and deploys a canopy in community videos (reported) |

### Safe Return (emergency autoland)

**Safe Return** is Cirrus's name for Garmin Emergency Autoland: the airplane lands itself when the pilot can't.

| Item | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| trigger | the red Safe Return button in the ceiling; it can also start automatically after emergency descent mode if the pilot stays unresponsive |
| what it does | picks an airport (runway length and width, fuel, crosswind, terrain, weather), broadcasts on the tuned frequency and 121.5 MHz, flies the approach, lowers gear and flaps, lands, brakes to a stop |
| cancel | press AP on the mode controller, or the AP disconnect button |
| in the sim | modeled; Sim Update 6 improved its destination choice, routing, gear, ice protection and braking |
| sim history | before SU6 it often couldn't find an airport (reported) |

### Emergency descent mode

**EDM** (emergency descent mode) is the Garmin feature that dives the jet to breathable air if you don't touch the
controls or panel for a while at high altitude, as in hypoxia. If you still don't respond after the descent, Safe
Return takes over. The exact thresholds are in the AFM and aren't public, and whether the sim models EDM is not
verified: don't rely on it.

## Recipes

### Cold and dark to takeoff

From a parking spot, engine off.

```text
Battery + avionics ON, parking brake SET, door closed
EFB: weights, fuel, plan -> avionics; note Vr
Throttle IDLE, ENGINE knob RUN, press START
Watch N1/ITT settle; CAS clean
Altitude preselect, heading sync, flaps 50%, trim takeoff
Taxi; on the runway: lights, transponder ALT
Arm A/T, press TOGA, throttle full forward
Rotate 85-90 KIAS, gear up, flaps up at 115, MCT
AP on after ~500 ft
```

### Quick IFR setup

For a plan already built in the EFB.

```text
EFB -> send to avionics; GTC Flight Plan: check it
GTC PROC: departure; preselect first altitude
Takeoff with TOGA + A/T; AP on, NAV + FLC (A/T FMS)
Cruise FL280-FL310
40+ NM before the 3-to-1 point: arrival + approach loaded
Preselect lower altitude, VNV
140 KIAS + flaps 50% by the IAF; APR
Gear down, flaps 100% by the FAF; Vref + 10
```

### Autoland demo (Safe Return)

See the emergency autoland land the jet for you. Start in cruise with the autopilot on.

```text
Cruise at FL200 or higher, AP on
Press the red Safe Return button in the ceiling
Watch the course swing toward the chosen airport
Hands off: gear, flaps, landing and braking are automatic
To cancel at any time: press AP or the AP disconnect
```

### Engine-out decision drill

Practice the CAPS altitude rules. Fail the engine in the sim's [failures menu](/aviation/msfs-2024/sim-basics/#failures).

```text
Failure below 600 ft AGL  -> land ahead, no CAPS, no turn back
Failure 600-2,000 ft AGL  -> pull CAPS now
Failure above 2,000 ft    -> glide to an airport, try a restart,
                             or slow to <= 135 KIAS and pull
```

### Early descent without speedbrakes

When ATC keeps you high.

```text
Throttle IDLE (A/T off, or MAN at a low speed)
Below 210 KIAS: gear DOWN for drag
Below 190 KIAS: flaps 50%
Ask ATC for lower early; no spoilers to save you
```
