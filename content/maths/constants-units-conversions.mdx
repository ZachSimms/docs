Values follow the 2019 SI definitions and CODATA 2018 recommendations. Quantities marked
_exact_ are defined, not measured. Grouped like appendix C of the _No bullshit guide_.

## Units

SI base units:

| Quantity            | Unit      | Symbol | Defined by                          |
| :------------------ | :-------- | :----- | :---------------------------------- |
| time                | second    | s      | caesium-133 hyperfine transition    |
| length              | metre     | m      | $c$                                 |
| mass                | kilogram  | kg     | $h$                                 |
| electric current    | ampere    | A      | $e$                                 |
| temperature         | kelvin    | K      | $k_B$                               |
| amount of substance | mole      | mol    | $N_A$                               |
| luminous intensity  | candela   | cd     | $K_{cd}$                            |

Derived units used in mechanics and electromagnetism:

| Quantity        | Unit    | Symbol | In base units                       |
| :-------------- | :------ | :----- | :---------------------------------- |
| frequency       | hertz   | Hz     | s⁻¹                                 |
| force           | newton  | N      | kg·m/s²                             |
| pressure        | pascal  | Pa     | N/m² = kg/(m·s²)                    |
| energy, work    | joule   | J      | N·m = kg·m²/s²                      |
| power           | watt    | W      | J/s = kg·m²/s³                      |
| charge          | coulomb | C      | A·s                                 |
| voltage         | volt    | V      | J/C = W/A                           |
| resistance      | ohm     | Ω      | V/A                                 |
| capacitance     | farad   | F      | C/V                                 |
| magnetic field  | tesla   | T      | N/(A·m) = kg/(A·s²)                 |
| angle           | radian  | rad    | m/m                                 |

Prefixes:

| Prefix | Symbol | Factor     | Prefix | Symbol | Factor    |
| :----- | :----- | :--------- | :----- | :----- | :-------- |
| tera   | T      | $10^{12}$  | milli  | m      | $10^{-3}$ |
| giga   | G      | $10^{9}$   | micro  | μ      | $10^{-6}$ |
| mega   | M      | $10^{6}$   | nano   | n      | $10^{-9}$ |
| kilo   | k      | $10^{3}$   | pico   | p      | $10^{-12}$ |

Temperature:

$$
T_K = T_C + 273.15,
\qquad
T_F = \tfrac{9}{5} T_C + 32,
\qquad
T_C = \tfrac{5}{9}(T_F - 32)
$$
