What to say on the radio, when to key the mic, and what ATC (air traffic control) will say back, phase by phase,
for US FAA procedures. The VFR sheets fly a Cessna 172, N123AB ("Skyhawk one two three alpha bravo"); the IFR
sheets fly a Cirrus SR22, N45CD ("Cirrus four five charlie delta"). Start with [radio fundamentals](/aviation/atc-comms/fundamentals/), then open the sheet for the
phase you're flying.

<Note kind="warning">For flight simulation and study. Not for real-world flight: use the current AIM, charts and your aircraft's POH/AFM.</Note>

| Phase of flight | Sheet |
| :--- | :--- |
| any call: structure, numbers, readbacks, key phrases, squawk codes, airspace, ICAO differences, MSFS ATC | [Radio fundamentals](/aviation/atc-comms/fundamentals/) |
| VFR at a towered field: ATIS, clearance delivery, ground, tower, flight following, Class B, C and D | [VFR at towered airports](/aviation/atc-comms/vfr-towered/) |
| VFR at a field with no tower (or a closed one): CTAF calls, pattern legs, flight plans with Flight Service | [VFR at untowered airports](/aviation/atc-comms/vfr-untowered/) |
| IFR clearance, taxi, takeoff and the departure frequency | [IFR departure](/aviation/atc-comms/ifr-departure/) |
| IFR with Center and Approach: altitudes, reroutes, approach clearances, canceling IFR | [IFR en route & arrival](/aviation/atc-comms/ifr-enroute-arrival/) |
| Mayday, pan-pan, squawk 7600 and lost-comms rules | [Emergencies & lost comms](/aviation/atc-comms/emergencies-lost-comms/) |
