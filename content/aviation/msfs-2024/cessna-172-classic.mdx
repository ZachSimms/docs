The steam-gauge Cessna 172 in MSFS 2024: a 172S Skyhawk SP with round gauges, GNS 530/430 (or Avidyne
IFD550/540) and a KAP 140 autopilot. It's the same airframe and engine as the
[172 G1000](/aviation/msfs-2024/cessna-172/), so the speeds are identical. What changes is the scan, the radios and
the autopilot. EFB, keys and assists are in [sim basics](/aviation/msfs-2024/sim-basics/), radio calls in
[ATC comms](/aviation/atc-comms/fundamentals/). Numbers come from the pre-G1000 Cessna 172S POH (172SPHUS,
revision 5, 2004) and FAA TCDS 3A12. Flows are paraphrased from that POH.

<Note kind="warning">For the simulator only. The checklists here are condensed paraphrases, not the Cessna 172S POH. Real
aircraft: use the manufacturer's POH/AFM and approved checklists.</Note>

## Speeds

Identical to the G1000 airplane. Same POH numbers, KIAS at 2,550 lb.

| Speed | KIAS | What it means |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Vs0 | 40 | stall, flaps 30°, bottom of the white arc |
| Vs1 | 48 | stall, flaps up, bottom of the green arc |
| Vr | 55 | lift the nosewheel |
| Vx | 62 (67 at 10,000 ft) | best angle of climb |
| Vy | 74 (72 at 10,000 ft) | best rate of climb |
| normal climb | 75–85 | enroute climb |
| best glide | 68 | engine out |
| engine failure after takeoff | 70 flaps up / 65 flaps down | also the power-off landing speeds |
| Va | 105 / 98 / 90 at 2,550 / 2,200 / 1,900 lb | full or abrupt inputs only below this |
| Vfe | 110 flaps 10°; 85 flaps 10°–30° | TCDS lists only 85[^vfe] |
| Vno | 129 | top of the green arc |
| Vne | 163 | red line |
| approach | 65–75 flaps up; 60–70 flaps 30° | normal landing |
| short-field approach | 61 | flaps 30° |
| go-around | 60 | full power, flaps 20° |

Sim vs real: we found no published stall or V-speed figures for the sim model. The SU3 notes list a sideslip drag
fix and new slip-ball physics for "Cessna 172 Skyhawk", so earlier builds flew slips differently.

## Cockpit & avionics

### Six-pack and scan

![Six-pack (generic FAA drawing): airspeed, attitude indicator and altimeter on top, turn coordinator, heading indicator and VSI below; the arrows show the radial scan out from the attitude indicator and back](/images/aviation/cessna-172-classic/six-pack.webp)
*Image: FAA, Instrument Flying Handbook (FAA-H-8083-15B) fig. 6-17, public domain, via [FAA](https://www.faa.gov/sites/faa.gov/files/regulations_policies/handbooks_manuals/aviation/FAA-H-8083-15B.pdf).*

| Position | Instrument | Powered by | Tells you |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| top left | airspeed indicator | pitot-static | speed; colored arcs as in Speeds |
| top center | attitude indicator (AI) | vacuum | pitch and bank |
| top right | altimeter | static | altitude; set BARO in the Kollsman window |
| bottom left | turn coordinator (TC) | electric | rate of turn and the slip ball |
| bottom center | directional gyro (DG), with heading bug | vacuum | heading; drifts, so reset to the compass |
| bottom right | vertical speed indicator (VSI) | static | climb or descent rate, with lag |

The scan (FAA Instrument Flying Handbook, radial scan): keep coming back to the attitude indicator, glance out to one
instrument, then back to the AI. The **primary** instrument is the one that shows the value you're holding:

| Maneuver | Pitch | Bank | Power |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| straight and level | altimeter | DG | airspeed |
| standard-rate turn (3°/s) | altimeter | turn coordinator | airspeed |
| constant-airspeed climb | airspeed | DG | tachometer (full power) |
| constant-rate descent | VSI | DG | airspeed |

| Engine and systems gauges | Normal |
| :--- | :--- |
| tachometer | green 2,100–2,500 RPM at sea level, to 2,600 at 5,000 ft, to 2,700 at 10,000 ft; red line 2,700 |
| vacuum gauge | 4.5–5.5 in.Hg green |
| oil pressure / oil temperature | 50–90 psi / 100–245 °F green |
| fuel flow | 0–12 gal/h green |
| where | oil, fuel quantity, vacuum/ammeter and EGT/fuel flow gauges sit left of the six-pack in the real airplane |
| annunciator panel | fuel, oil pressure, low vacuum, low voltage (VOLTS) |

### Radios and GPS

![Generic FAA drawing of a radio stack, not the GNS 530/430 of the sim: two COM/NAV radios with active and standby frequencies, an ADF, a GPS, and a transponder at the bottom with the IDT key, code digits and OFF/SBY/ON/ALT knob](/images/aviation/cessna-172-classic/radio-stack.webp)
*Image: FAA, Instrument Flying Handbook (FAA-H-8083-15B) fig. 2-1, public domain, via [FAA](https://www.faa.gov/sites/faa.gov/files/regulations_policies/handbooks_manuals/aviation/FAA-H-8083-15B.pdf).*

| Unit | Role | Key controls |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| GNS 530 (top) | COM1, NAV1, GPS1 (usual setup) | big/small knobs; COM and VLOC flip-flop; **CDI** key switches the nav needle between GPS and VLOC (VOR/localizer) |
| GNS 430 (below) | COM2, NAV2, GPS2 | same keys on a smaller screen |
| common keys | D→, FPL, PROC, MENU, CLR, ENT, OBS, MSG | same meanings as on the G1000 |
| IFD550 + IFD540 (SU5 variants) | replace the two GNS units | touchscreen plus knobs; FMS, MAP and AUX pages |
| KAP 140 | 2-axis autopilot (roll and pitch) | see below |
| transponder, ADF | squawk code and mode; ADF tuning | present in the sim; exact models not verified |

### Differences from the G1000 172

| Area | Classic | G1000 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| flight instruments | six-pack; AI and DG vacuum-driven | PFD with AHRS (solid-state gyros); vacuum drives only the standby attitude indicator |
| heading | DG drifts, reset to the compass | AHRS heading, no drift |
| engine gauges | round gauges, left sub-panel | MFD engine strip with exceedance colors |
| nav | GNS 530/430 or IFD, CDI needle(s) with OBS | G1000 NXi HSI with CDI softkey, synthetic map |
| autopilot | KAP 140: ROL, HDG, NAV, APR, REV, ALT, VS | GFC 700: adds FLC, VNAV, flight director, BC |
| altitude preselect | on the KAP 140 display | ALT SEL on the PFD |
| standby power | none; battery only | standby battery for at least 30 min |
| start | ignition key, no STBY BATT test | STBY BATT test, then key |
| electrical failure | lose the radios, GPS and autopilot; vacuum instruments keep working | PFD stays on the standby battery |
| learning value | instrument scan, partial panel | automation and flight-plan management |

## Normal procedures

Condensed from the 172S POH (non-G1000). Same order as the G1000 sheet, minus the standby battery and plus
round-gauge checks.

### Preflight

| Item | Action / value |
| :--- | :--- |
| weight, fuel | EFB: 2,550 lb max, 53 gal usable |
| walkaround | covers, chocks, tie-downs off (as the variant shows them) |
| DG | will be set to the compass after start |

### Before start

![Fuel selector on the floor between the seats (generic FAA drawing; the gallons on the 172 placard differ): turn it to BOTH before start](/images/aviation/cessna-172-classic/fuel-selector.webp)

### Engine start

![Split master switch (ALT and BAT halves) and the ignition key from an FAA starting-circuit drawing (generic): master on, then turn the key to START and let go when the engine fires](/images/aviation/cessna-172-classic/master-and-ignition.webp)
*Image: FAA, Pilot's Handbook of Aeronautical Knowledge (FAA-H-8083-25C) fig. 7-20, public domain, via [FAA](https://www.faa.gov/regulations_policies/handbooks_manuals/aviation/phak).*

| Item | Action / value |
| :--- | :--- |
| throttle | open ¼ inch |
| mixture | idle cutoff |
| prop area | clear |
| master switch, beacon | on |
| prime (cold only) | aux fuel pump on, mixture full rich 3–5 s until fuel flow shows, mixture cutoff, pump off |
| ignition key | START; release when it fires |
| mixture | smoothly to rich |
| oil pressure | rising into the green |
| nav lights, avionics master, radios | on |
| flaps | retract |

### Before taxi / taxi

| Item | Action / value |
| :--- | :--- |
| ATIS, altimeter | Kollsman window set, compare field elevation |
| GNS/IFD | COM1 ground, COM2 tower or ATIS; flight plan loaded; CDI on GPS or VLOC |
| DG | set to the compass (or runway heading on the runway) |
| KAP 140 | self-test done (real unit: runs at power-up); BARO matches the altimeter |
| transponder | code set |
| taxi | brakes checked; in turns, TC and DG turn the right way, ball swings out, AI stays level |

### Run-up

![Combined vacuum gauge and ammeter left of the six-pack (generic FAA drawing): at the 1,800 RPM run-up check suction in the 4.5–5.5 in.Hg green band and the ammeter at or above zero](/images/aviation/cessna-172-classic/vacuum-ammeter.webp)
*Image: FAA, Instrument Flying Handbook (FAA-H-8083-15B) fig. 11-4, public domain, via [FAA](https://www.faa.gov/sites/faa.gov/files/regulations_policies/handbooks_manuals/aviation/FAA-H-8083-15B.pdf).*

| Item | Action / value |
| :--- | :--- |
| parking brake, doors, belts | set, closed, secure |
| flight controls | free and correct |
| flight instruments | checked and set |
| fuel quantity, mixture, selector | checked, rich, BOTH |
| 1,800 RPM | mag drop ≤ 150 RPM each, ≤ 50 RPM difference; vacuum 4.5–5.5; engine gauges and ammeter checked |
| annunciators | none lit |
| idle | check, then ≤ 1,000 RPM |
| radios and avionics, NAV/GPS source | set |
| autopilot | **off** (the KAP 140 must be off for takeoff and landing) |
| electric trim | check if installed |
| elevator trim, flaps | takeoff mark; 0°–10° |
| brakes | release |

| Item | Action / value |
| :--- | :--- |
| power | full throttle; mixture rich (above 3,000 ft, lean for max RPM) |
| rotate | 55 KIAS |
| climb | 70–80 KIAS; flaps up |

| Item | Action / value |
| :--- | :--- |

## Flying it in the sim

| Step | Action |
| :--- | :--- |
| plan | EFB → **SEND TO AVIONICS**. If your variant doesn't take it, enter the route on the GNS: FPL, then waypoints with the small and big knobs. |
| check | FPL page on the GNS 530: departure, waypoints, approach |
| nav source | **CDI** key: GPS for the flight plan, VLOC for a VOR or ILS (tune it on the VLOC side) |
| heading bug | on the DG; in NAV, APR and REV modes it also gives the autopilot its course reference, so set it to the course |
| altitude preselect | KAP 140 rotary knobs; the autopilot captures it when climbing or descending in VS |
| engage | trimmed, above the pattern: AP (engages in ROL + VS at the current rate), then HDG or NAV |

KAP 140 controls (Bendix/King supplement in the 172S POH):

| Control | Does |
| :--- | :--- |
| AP | engage/disengage; starts in ROL (wing leveler) + VS (holds the current vertical speed) |
| HDG | follow the DG heading bug; press again to go back to ROL |
| NAV | capture and track the #1 CDI source: VOR, localizer or GPS |
| APR | approach: tighter tracking of VOR, GPS or localizer, plus glideslope capture on an ILS |
| REV | back-course localizer; no glideslope |
| ALT | hold the altitude you're at when you press it (overshoots about 10% of the current VS) |
| UP / DN | VS mode: ±100 fpm per press (held: 300 fpm per second). ALT mode: ±20 ft per press |
| rotary knobs, ARM | set the preselect altitude; arming for capture is automatic when you set it |
| BARO | shows the autopilot's baro setting for 3 s; set it to match the altimeter |
| A/P DISC (yoke) | disconnect; two-second tone and a flashing AP |

The KAP 140 has no FLC and no VNAV. In a climb, hold airspeed yourself with VS: start at +500 fpm and cut it back
if the speed drops below about 75 KIAS.

**Approach setup.** GNS: PROC → select the approach → Load, then Activate when cleared. ILS: tune the localizer on
the 530's VLOC, press **CDI** until the needle shows VLOC, set the heading bug to the inbound course, then APR on the
KAP 140. GPS approach: the CDI stays on GPS, APR on the KAP 140.

## Emergencies

![Split MASTER rocker (generic FAA drawing): ALT half on the left, BAT half on the right; for an overcharge switch off only the ALT half](/images/aviation/cessna-172-classic/master-switch.webp)
*Image: FAA, Instrument Flying Handbook (FAA-H-8083-15B) fig. 11-5, public domain, via [FAA](https://www.faa.gov/sites/faa.gov/files/regulations_policies/handbooks_manuals/aviation/FAA-H-8083-15B.pdf).*

Immediate-action items are **bold**. Paraphrased from the 172S POH section 3.

| Situation | Flow |
| :--- | :--- |
| engine failure on the takeoff roll | **throttle idle, brakes, flaps up**; mixture cutoff, ignition off, master off |
| engine failure right after takeoff | **70 KIAS flaps up / 65 flaps down**; mixture cutoff, fuel shutoff off, ignition off, flaps as needed, master off, door unlatched, land straight ahead |
| engine failure in flight | **68 KIAS**; fuel shutoff on, selector BOTH, aux fuel pump on, mixture rich, ignition BOTH (START if the prop stopped); pump off once running (back on if fuel flow drops to zero) |
| engine fire in flight | **mixture cutoff, fuel shutoff off, aux pump off, master off**; cabin heat and air off; 100 KIAS; forced landing |
| electrical fire in flight | **master off**; vents and heat closed; extinguisher; then avionics master and all switches off |
| ammeter pegged high (overcharge) | alternator half of the master off; non-essential equipment off; land as soon as practical |
| low voltage (VOLTS) light | avionics master off, alternator field breaker in, master off then on, check the light is out, avionics back on; if it comes back: alternator off, shed load, land as soon as practical |
| vacuum failure | check vacuum gauge; if low, the AI and DG will fail: cover them, fly the turn coordinator, airspeed, altimeter and compass |
| lost in cloud, no vacuum | note the compass heading, standard-rate left turn on the TC for 60 s by the clock, roll level, check the compass shows the reciprocal |
| autopilot runs away | **grasp the yoke, press and hold A/P DISC**; avionics master off if it won't let go; don't re-engage |

**Memory items**: best glide 68 · failure after takeoff 70/65 · fire: mixture, fuel, master · autopilot: yoke and
disconnect · squawk 7700. Calls: [emergencies and lost comms](/aviation/atc-comms/emergencies-lost-comms/).

## Recipes

### Cold and dark to takeoff
The whole flow in one pass.
1. Brakes set, breakers in, avionics master off, selector BOTH, fuel shutoff in.
2. Throttle ¼ inch, mixture cutoff, master and beacon on.
3. Cold engine: aux pump on, mixture rich 3–5 s, cutoff, pump off.
4. Key to START, mixture rich, oil pressure up, avionics master on, radios on, flaps up.
5. ATIS, altimeter, DG to the compass, GNS plan, squawk. Taxi and check the instruments in turns.
6. Run-up at 1,800 RPM: mags, vacuum, gauges. KAP 140 off, trim set, flaps 0°–10°.
7. Full power, 55 KIAS rotate, 70–80 KIAS climb.

### Pattern work
Same numbers as the [G1000 pattern](/aviation/msfs-2024/cessna-172/#pattern-work). On downwind, check the DG
against the runway heading and fly the airspeed indicator, not the attitude indicator.

### Quick IFR setup
1. EFB plan → **SEND TO AVIONICS AND ATC**, or enter it on the GNS 530. Check the FPL page.
2. Altimeter and KAP 140 BARO set, preselect the initial altitude, heading bug on the runway heading.
3. CDI on GPS. Clearance per [ifr-departure](/aviation/atc-comms/ifr-departure/).
4. After takeoff: AP (ROL + VS), set +500 fpm with UP/DN, then HDG or NAV.
5. For the approach: PROC → load, CDI to VLOC for an ILS, heading bug on the inbound course, APR.

### Partial-panel practice
1. Turn gyro drift compensation off.
2. At a safe altitude, cover the AI and DG (a sticky note on the screen works).
3. Fly headings with the turn coordinator and compass. Compass turning error: roll out early when turning to
   north, late when turning to south.
4. Do a timed 180° turn: standard rate for 60 s.
