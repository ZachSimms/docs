The MSFS 2024 Cirrus Vision Jet: a single-engine, single-pilot personal jet with autothrottle, a whole-airplane
parachute and Safe Return emergency autoland. It is free in every edition. Limits and procedures come from the Cirrus
Airplane Flight Manual (AFM, P/N 31452-001) and Pilot's Information Manual (PIM, P/N 31453-001). That AFM covers
earlier serials with Perspective Touch avionics and an FL280 ceiling. Where the G2 in the sim differs (FL310, more
cabin pressure, Safe Return), the sheet uses the type certificate (FAA A00018CH, mirrored by EASA.IM.A.615) and notes
the difference. Cruise and fuel numbers for the G2 come from Cirrus and pilot reports. Speeds are KIAS unless marked.
Editions, the EFB, SimBrief, the checklist panel and key bindings are in [sim basics](/aviation/msfs-2024/sim-basics/);
radio calls are in [ATC comms](/aviation/atc-comms/); the piston sibling is the
[SR22T](/aviation/msfs-2024/cirrus-sr22/).

<Note kind="warning">For the simulator only. These checklists are condensed paraphrases of a Cirrus AFM for other
serials, mixed with sim flows, not the AFM itself. Never use them to fly a real airplane.</Note>

## Profile

The sim's jet is FlightFX's "Vision Jet G2", shipped free in MSFS 2024 and maintained by Working Title in sim updates.
Cirrus has since moved on to the G2+ (2021) and G3 (2026), so current Cirrus spec pages quote slightly higher numbers.

| Item | Value | Notes and source |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| role | single-engine very light jet, pressurized, retractable gear | certified for one pilot (AFM §2) |
| engine | Williams FJ33-5A turbofan, 1,846 lb takeoff thrust | TCDS; AFM §1; takeoff thrust limited to 5 min |
| seats | up to 7 (pilot and 6), minimum crew 1 | TCDS; AFM §2 |
| max takeoff weight | 6,000 lb; ramp 6,040, landing 5,550, zero fuel 4,900 | TCDS; AFM §2 |
| usable fuel | 296 US gal (2,001 lb) of Jet A, 148 per wing | AFM §2; max imbalance 15 gal |
| typical burn | about 60–64 gal/h (400–430 lb/h) at FL310 | Aviation Week and AOPA pilot reports (G2)[^1] |
| cruise | about 305 KTAS at FL310; about 311–313 KTAS max cruise lower down[^1] | AOPA (G2); FlightFX manual |
| ceiling | FL310 (31,000 ft) on the G2; FL280 on earlier serials | TCDS; AFM §2 gives FL280 for its serials[^5] |
| range | about 1,100–1,275 NM, depending on load and speed[^2] | AOPA (G2); Cirrus |
| pressurization | 7.1 psi differential: 8,000 ft cabin at FL310 | AOPA (G2); AFM §2 gives 6.4 psid for FL280 serials |
| airports | elevation up to 10,000 ft; tailwind up to 10 kt | AFM §2 |
| MSFS edition | Standard (so in every edition) | first-party aircraft list; Fly Away Simulation; MSFS Addons |
| avionics | Cirrus Perspective Touch+ (Garmin G3000-based), autothrottle, ESP, Safe Return | touch controllers under the displays |

**KTAS** is true airspeed. **FL310** is flight level 310: 31,000 ft on the standard altimeter setting (29.92 inHg),
used from 18,000 ft up. **ESP** (electronic stability and protection) nudges the controls back when you exceed bank,
pitch or speed limits.

## Speeds

Limits are from AFM §2 and match the TCDS. Takeoff and landing speeds are from AFM §4; the EFB's takeoff and landing
performance (added in Sim Update 2) gives numbers for your weight.

| Speed | KIAS | Meaning and notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Vmo | 250 | maximum operating speed (red and white "barber pole" on the tape) |
| Mmo | 0.53 Mach | maximum operating Mach number; limits you before 250 KIAS up high |
| Vo | 150 | operating maneuvering speed: full control inputs only below this; also the descent limit in turbulence |
| Vfe 50% | 190 | max speed with flaps 50% (15°); no flaps above 17,500 ft |
| Vfe 100% | 150 | max speed with flaps 100% (39°) |
| Vlo extend / Vle | 210 | max speed to lower the gear, and with the gear down |
| Vlo retract | 150 | max speed to raise the gear |
| Vr | 85 | rotate, then 10° nose up (AFM §4)[^3] |
| Vx, flaps 50% | 91 | best angle of climb with gear up, flaps 50%; the go-around target (AFM §5) |
| flaps up | 115 | flaps 50% → UP after takeoff, clear of obstacles (AFM §4) |
| climb | about 190 falling to 140 | MCT climb schedule: about 175–190 KIAS low, 140 KIAS by FL280 (AFM §5 tables) |
| approach | 140 | flaps 50% for the approach (AFM §4) |
| Vref, flaps 100% | 71–89 | 71 at 4,000 lb up to 85 at 5,500 lb and 89 at 6,000 lb; fly Vref + 10 on final (AFM §4) |
| Vref, flaps 50% | 82–100 | same weights; 96 at 5,500 lb |
| stall, full flaps | 67 KCAS | at 6,000 lb, wings level, idle; 60 at 4,800 lb (AFM §5) |
| best glide | 114 / 104 / 93 | at 6,000 / 5,000 / 4,000 lb, gear and flaps up (AFM §3) |
| minimum maneuvering | Vref + 20 / + 15 / + 10 | flaps UP / 50% / 100%, for up to 40° of bank (AFM §2) |
| icing | 160 | minimum speed in icing conditions, clean (except climb, approach and landing) |
| CAPS | 135 KIAS and 145 KTAS | whichever is lower; pulled faster, the autopilot slows the jet first (AFM §3) |

**Vref** is the reference landing speed at the 50-ft point, 1.3 × the stall speed; the green donut on the airspeed tape
shows it for your configuration. Flaps UP, Vref is 89–109 KIAS; with an iced airframe Vref rises by about 15–30 kt and
you land with flaps 50% (AFM §4 table).

Autothrottle (A/T) modes on the real G2 and in the sim (the AFM for earlier serials has no autothrottle steps):

| Mode | Does |
| :--- | :--- |
| MAN | holds the speed you set with the speed control (thumbwheel or bug) |
| FMS | follows the flight plan's speed schedule for climb, cruise, descent and approach |
| MCT | throttle "snaps" to maximum continuous thrust so you don't over-temp the engine (FlightFX manual) |
| protection | A/T prevents over- and under-speeds together with ESP |

There are **no speedbrakes**: the gear (below 210 KIAS) and flaps (below 190 KIAS and 17,500 ft) are the only drag.
Plan descents early.

## Flying it in the sim

A typical IFR flight. Load the plan as in [sim basics](/aviation/msfs-2024/sim-basics/#flight-planning-efb-and-simbrief); calls are in
[IFR departure](/aviation/atc-comms/ifr-departure/) and [IFR en route and arrival](/aviation/atc-comms/ifr-enroute-arrival/).

| Step | What to do |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1. plan | EFB or SimBrief plan sent to the avionics; check it on the GTC Flight Plan page |
| 2. performance | EFB takeoff data for Vr; GTC PERF for weights if shown |
| 3. departure | GTC PROC: departure; altitude preselect set; heading synced; TO/GA pressed |
| 4. takeoff | flaps 50%, trim in the green band, A/T armed, thrust T/O, rotate at 85, gear up, flaps up at 115 |
| 5. climb | AP on above 400 ft; FLC or VNV; A/T FMS; MCT |
| 6. cruise | FL280–FL310; A/T holds the FMS or manual speed |
| 7. descent | arrival and approach loaded early; preselect the lower altitude; VNV |
| 8. approach | 140 KIAS and flaps 50%; APR; gear down, then flaps 100% and Vref + 10 on final |
| 9. landing | AP off before landing; Vref at the threshold |

Autopilot modes:

| Mode | Does |
| :--- | :--- |
| HDG | follows the heading bug |
| NAV | follows the FMS or VOR/LOC course |
| APR | arms the approach: ILS, or GPS lateral and vertical guidance |
| ALT | holds altitude |
| VS | holds a vertical speed |
| FLC | flight level change: holds an airspeed by pitch while the A/T sets power |
| VNV | follows the FMS vertical path and altitude constraints (VNAV) |
| LVL | levels the wings and stops any climb or descent: the button passengers are briefed on (AFM §10) |

Autopilot limits (AFM §2):

| Limit | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| minimum speed | 105 KIAS clean, 95 with flaps 50%, 85 with flaps 100% |
| maximum speed | 250 KIAS / Mach 0.53 clean, 210 gear down, 190 flaps 50%, 150 flaps 100% |
| minimum use height | 400 ft AGL after takeoff; 1,000 ft en route; 200 ft or DA on a GS or GP approach; 400 ft or MDA otherwise |
| overspeed protection | above 253 KIAS or Mach 0.54 the autopilot pitches up (MAXSPD on the PFD) (PIM ch. 17) |

Flight-level change: set the new level, press FLC, and the A/T moves the throttle to climb or descent power. For a
descent, the rule of thumb is 3 NM per 1,000 ft: FL310 to 3,000 ft needs about 84 NM.

## Emergencies

Condensed from AFM 31452-001 §3 (emergency) and §10 (CAPS guidance). The AFM marks memory items; the red CAS messages
each have their own checklist there.

![Overhead emergency panel: 1 the guarded ENGINE FIRE ACKNOWLEDGE switch, 2 the two PUSH TO DISCHARGE buttons, the CABIN PRESSURE DUMP button, EMER BATT, the ELT switch, MASK MIC switches and the CAPS placard (pull the handle down steadily with both hands)](/images/aviation/vision-jet/emergency-panel.webp)
*Image: Cirrus Aircraft, Vision SF50 Pilot's Information Manual (P/N 31453-001) fig. 9-4, © Cirrus Aircraft, all rights reserved, reproduced for reference.*

| Emergency | What to do |
| :--- | :--- |
| rejected takeoff | maximum braking without skidding, thrust IDLE; don't set the parking brake; brakes need a cool-down |
| engine problem below Vr | as above, plus ENGINE knob OFF and press the button; on fire, the ground fire drill below |
| engine failure after takeoff, low | 100 KIAS, gear and flaps as required, land straight ahead turning only to miss obstacles; if time, knob OFF, CABIN PRESSURE DUMP, GENs and BATs OFF |
| engine failure below 600 ft AGL | land straight ahead if practicable; if not, pull CAPS at once (AFM §10) |
| engine failure 600–2,000 ft AGL | pull CAPS immediately (AFM §10) |
| engine failure above 2,000 ft AGL | pitch for 120–200 KIAS, turn toward a landing site, masks on above 15,000 ft; air start if the engine looks safe; by 1,000 ft AGL, either the landing is assured or you pull CAPS |
| air start | 120–200 KIAS below 18,000 ft; thrust IDLE, GENs OFF, fuel to the other tank; knob OFF and press the button; wait for N2 below 24% (16% is better); knob RUN, press the button; GENs ON if it runs |
| engine fire in flight | thrust IDLE, pitch for about 120 KIAS, wait 15 s; still on: lift the guard and toggle ENGINE FIRE ACKNOWLEDGE, push one PUSH TO DISCHARGE, mask 100%, knob OFF and press the button, GENs OFF, land without power or pull CAPS |
| fire warning goes out by itself | probably a bleed leak: least thrust needed, land as soon as possible, BLEED to FRESH |
| engine fire on the ground | ENGINE FIRE ACKNOWLEDGE toggled, a PUSH TO DISCHARGE button, knob OFF and press the button, evacuate |
| cabin altitude high | masks 100%, BLEED verified, mic to MASK MIC, emergency descent as needed, check passenger oxygen, squawk 7700 |
| emergency descent | AP DISC, thrust IDLE, pitch about 20° nose down, accelerate up to 250 KIAS / Mach 0.53 (150 in turbulence) |
| both generators out (EMER BUS VOLTS) | GENs OFF, interior lights OFF, BATs ON, EMER BATT ON, land as soon as possible; about 60 min of battery; no autopilot, MFD or GTC 3, gear by alternate extension |
| stall warning or stick pusher | lower the angle of attack, thrust T/O; don't fight the pusher |
| gear won't come down | below 210 KIAS: LANDING GEAR HYD breaker (B4) out, handle DOWN, pull the alternate extension handle to its stop, check three green |

For the radio side (Mayday, 7700, lost comms), see [emergencies and lost comms](/aviation/atc-comms/emergencies-lost-comms/).

Gliding (AFM §3): best glide is 114 KIAS at 6,000 lb, 104 at 5,000 lb and 93 at 4,000 lb (the clean green donut),
and the glide ratio is about 14.7 to 1, roughly 2.4 NM per 1,000 ft. For a power-off landing, fly high key at
3,000 ft AGL (115–130 KIAS), low key at 1,500 ft (120), base at 1,000 ft (120). Keep at least 110 KIAS until short
final, and lower the gear and flaps only once the landing is assured.

### CAPS

| Item | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| what | Cirrus Airframe Parachute System: a rocket pulls a parachute out of the nose and the jet descends under it |
| handle | the CAPS T-handle in the ceiling console, next to the emergency panel (PIM fig. 3-2) |
| max speed | below 135 KIAS and 145 KTAS, whichever is lower (higher up, true airspeed is the tighter limit) |
| pulled fast | the autopilot's CAPS mode levels the wings, pitches up and idles the engine to slow down: about 15–24 s from 250 KIAS; it deploys after about 30 s whatever the speed. Don't press AP DISC (AFM §3) |
| no CAPS autopilot | with a CAPS AUTOPILOT INOP message, slow down yourself and set IDLE before you pull |
| altitude | no minimum set; horizontal deployments lost under 600 ft; above 1,000 ft AGL is advisable (AFM §10) |
| descent | about 1,560 fpm under the canopy; touchdown is like a drop from about 10.5 ft (AFM §10) |
| after the pull | masks 100% above 15,000 ft; ENGINE FIRE ACKNOWLEDGE toggled (shuts off the fuel); ELT on; gear UP; after radio calls, BAT and GEN OFF below 15,000 ft; loose items secured, belts tight, brace; out upwind once stopped |
| gear | leave it UP: a CAPS landing with the gear down may puncture a fuel tank (AFM §10) |
| in the sim | the handle works and deploys a canopy in community videos (reported) |

### Safe Return (emergency autoland)

**Safe Return** is Cirrus's name for Garmin Emergency Autoland: the airplane lands itself when the pilot can't. It is a
G2 feature, so the AFM for earlier serials doesn't cover it; the notes below are from Cirrus, AOPA and the FAA FSB
report.

| Item | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| trigger | the red Safe Return button in the ceiling; it can also start automatically after emergency descent mode if the pilot stays unresponsive |
| what it does | picks an airport (runway length and width, fuel, crosswind, terrain, weather), broadcasts on the tuned frequency and 121.5 MHz, flies the approach, lowers gear and flaps, lands, brakes to a stop |
| cancel | press AP on the mode controller, or the AP disconnect button |
| in the sim | modeled; Sim Update 6 improved its destination choice, routing, gear, ice protection and braking |
| sim history | before SU6 it often couldn't find an airport (reported) |

### Emergency descent mode

**EDM** (emergency descent mode) dives the jet to breathable air after a loss of cabin pressure (PIM ch. 17). It
works only with the autopilot already on. It starts when you are at or above 14,900 ft, the CABIN ALTITUDE HIGH warning
has been on for 15 s, and no manual cabin dump is in progress. The red AUTO DESCENT warning sounds and EDM shows above
the airspeed tape. The autopilot sets 14,000 ft and a target 10 kt below the speed limit, idles the thrust, then levels
at 14,000 ft and sets about 83% N1. Only AP DISC cancels it, and the CABIN PRESSURE DUMP button disables it. On the G2,
Safe Return follows if the pilot still doesn't respond (FSB report). Whether the sim models EDM is not verified: don't
rely on it.

## Recipes

### Cold and dark to takeoff

From a parking spot, engine off.

```text
Gear handle DOWN, FUEL CONTROL AUTO, door latched
BAT 1 + BAT 2 ON, GEN 1 + GEN 2 ON
EFB: weights, fuel, plan -> avionics; note Vr
Strobes ON, thrust IDLE, brakes held
ENGINE knob RUN, press ENGINE START/STOP
Watch N1/N2/ITT settle; CAS clean
CAPS pin out; BLEED switch to BLEED
Altitude preselect, heading sync, flaps 50%
Trim in the green band; TO/GA (FD 10 deg)
Taxi; on the runway: lights, transponder ALT
Arm A/T, thrust T/O, rotate 85, pitch 10 deg
Gear up, flaps up at 115, MCT, AP on above 400 ft
```

### Quick IFR setup

For a plan already built in the EFB.

```text
EFB -> send to avionics; GTC Flight Plan: check it
GTC PROC: departure; preselect first altitude
Takeoff with TOGA + A/T; AP on, NAV + FLC (A/T FMS)
Cruise FL280-FL310
40+ NM before the 3-to-1 point: arrival + approach
Preselect lower altitude, VNV
140 KIAS + flaps 50% for the approach; APR
Gear down, flaps 100%, Vref + 10; AP off to land
```

### Autoland demo (Safe Return)

See the emergency autoland land the jet for you. Start in cruise with the autopilot on.

```text
Cruise at FL200 or higher, AP on
Press the red Safe Return button in the ceiling
Watch the course swing toward the chosen airport
Hands off: gear, flaps, landing and braking are automatic
To cancel at any time: press AP or the AP disconnect
```

### Engine-out decision drill

Practice the CAPS altitude rules from AFM §10. Fail the engine in the sim's
[failures menu](/aviation/msfs-2024/sim-basics/#failures).

```text
Below 600 ft AGL  -> land ahead; CAPS if you can't
600-2,000 ft AGL  -> pull CAPS now
Above 2,000 ft    -> 120-200 KIAS, air start below
                     18,000 ft, glide to a runway
By 1,000 ft AGL   -> landing assured, or CAPS
CAPS: under 135 KIAS/145 KTAS, IDLE, gear UP
```

### Early descent without speedbrakes

When ATC keeps you high.

```text
Thrust IDLE (A/T off, or MAN at a low speed)
Below 210 KIAS: gear DOWN for drag
Below 190 KIAS and 17,500 ft: flaps 50%
Ask ATC for lower early; no spoilers to save you
```
