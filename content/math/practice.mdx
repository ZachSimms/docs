Problems written for you by an AI, on any topic and at the level you pick, checked as you answer.
Coding exercises live in the [playground](/playground/?mode=exercise).

## How it works

<Steps>
<Step title="Ask for a problem">
Pick an area and a difficulty, or describe what you want to practice ("chain rule derivatives",
"percent increase word problems"), then press Generate problem.
</Step>
<Step title="Answer it">
Type the final answer in the form the problem asks for: `3/4`, `2√3`, `x = 2, x = -3`. Under the
box, "reads as" shows how a numeric answer was understood; "how to type answers" lists what works.
</Step>
<Step title="Check">
Numeric answers are checked instantly, in your browser. Expressions and words are checked by the
AI, which accepts any equivalent form.
</Step>
<Step title="Learn from a miss">
Take a hint, or add your working and press "explain my mistake": the AI points at the first step
that goes wrong without giving the answer away. The worked solution is there when you want it.
</Step>
</Steps>

Problems and your progress stay in this browser, under Recent: × removes one, "clear all" removes
them all.
